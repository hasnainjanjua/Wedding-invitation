#### 1. Hardcoded Monogram in Wax Seals (Template Reusability Bug)
* **Location:** [Lines 95, 169](file:///d:/MEGAPROJECT/wedding-inviation/Wedding-invitation/wedding-invitation.html#L95)
* **Issue:** While `$('mono').textContent = C.groom[0] + '&' + C.bride[0]` dynamically sets the letter monogram, the `.seal` and `.seal-start` elements have **hardcoded `"Z&R"`** in the HTML. Changing the names in `const C` will leave the wax seal displaying `"Z&R"`.

